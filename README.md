# Project-5-Predicting-Depression
Predicting Depression from Mental Health Survey Data using Deep Learning

**Objective:**

- The objective is to predict whether an individual may experience depression based on various factors such as demographic information, lifestyle choices, and medical history.
- Using deep learning, the model will be able to identify patterns in the data that could indicate a higher likelihood of depression.
- Implementing a Streamlit app with best model for mental health prediction.
- Deployed the Streamlit app in Streamlit cloud community.

**Business Use Cases:**
- Healthcare Providers can use this model to identify patients at risk of depression early, enabling timely interventions and better care.
- Through Corporate Wellness Programs, Companies can incorporate this model to monitor the mental health of their employees and offer mental health support proactively.
- Government organizations and NGOs can use the model to identify population groups at risk of depression and allocate mental health resources effectively.
